// About

The game that fights back.

And This Game Hates You Back is a precision rage platformer where the levels aren't just hard — they're personal. Navigate brutal spike-filled stages as Lilguy, a 2 kilogram cosmic deity with the power to unmake the universe and absolutely no idea how to use it.

Every death is accompanied by Unhelpful Bot, your AI companion who exists solely to make you feel worse about yourself. He has a lot of material to work with.

The free demo includes the first 5 levels. The full game is £2.99. Good luck. You'll need it.

  • Goreless-but-gory death animations (Lilguy bleeds a lot)
